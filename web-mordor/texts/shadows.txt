REPORT: EASTERN BORDERS
YEAR: 3018 T.A.

Scouts report increasing movement in the Ash Mountains. 
The skies over Barad-dûr are darkening. 
There are whispers of a new power rising, or perhaps an old one returning.
Traces of distinct boot prints found near the Morannon, but they disappear into the rocky terrain.
We must double the watch.

- Captain of the Watch
