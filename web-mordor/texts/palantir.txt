INVENTORY: THE SEVEN STONES

1. Osgiliath - LOST in the Anduin.
2. Minas Ithil - CAPTURED. Now resides in Barad-dûr.
3. Orthanc - Held by Saruman the White (Corrupted?).
4. Minas Tirith - Denethor watches, but sees only despair.
5. Amon Sûl - LOST.
6. Annúminas - LOST.
7. Elostirion - Looks only West.

The Master sees all through the Ithil Stone. None can hide from his gaze.
